## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes management direction and mandatory principles for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and supporting the organization’s information security management system (ISMS). It sets expectations for governance, risk-based decision-making, implementation, monitoring and continual improvement.

## 2. Scope

The organization shall define the ISMS scope in its approved scope statement. This policy applies to personnel, processes, information, technology, facilities and third parties to the extent they fall within that scope or interact with in-scope information assets. The organization shall document relevant interfaces, dependencies and exclusions rather than assuming universal applicability.

## 3. Terms and Definitions

| Term | Working Definition (Model) |
|---|---|
| **Information security** | Preservation of the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information. |
| **ISMS** | The policies, processes, roles, resources and controls the organization uses to establish, operate, monitor and improve information security within its approved scope. |
| **Risk owner** | The person or role with accountability and authority to manage an assigned risk, including treatment decisions and acceptance within delegated limits. |
| **Control owner** | The person or role responsible for implementing, operating and evidencing an assigned control. |
| **Exception** | A documented, risk-assessed and authorized deviation from a mandatory requirement that is time-bound or subject to periodic review. |
| **Nonconformity** | Failure to fulfil a requirement of this policy, a supporting controlled document, or another applicable obligation. |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of a nonconformity and prevent recurrence, verified as effective before closure. |
| **Evidence** | Attributable, retrievable and protected records demonstrating that a requirement or control was performed as defined. |

## 4. Policy Objectives

- Protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss and disruption in a manner proportionate to its classification and risk.
- Establish clear accountability for information security risks, controls, exceptions and remediation.
- Identify and assess information security risks using an approved, repeatable methodology and maintain treatment decisions and risk acceptance records.
- Meet applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and other obligations that have been identified and validated for the organization.
- Ensure security requirements are considered in personnel lifecycle, supplier relationships, system acquisition, development, operation and change.
- Evaluate control operation and ISMS performance using defined evidence, monitoring, audit and management review processes.

## 5. Governance and Accountability

| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| **Top management / executive sponsor** | Approve policy and ISMS direction; assign responsibilities; consider resources; review significant risks, performance and improvement needs. |
| **ISMS owner / Information Security Manager** | Coordinate ISMS operation, policy maintenance, risk and control reporting, awareness, internal audit coordination and corrective-action follow-up. |
| **Risk owners** | Assess assigned risks, select or recommend treatment, maintain action plans, and seek acceptance only from an authorized role. |
| **Control owners / process owners** | Implement assigned controls, maintain operating procedures and records, report failures and provide evidence for monitoring and audit. |
| **IT and engineering teams** | Implement approved technical requirements and retain operational logs and change records relevant to assigned controls. |
| **People managers** | Ensure personnel understand role-specific responsibilities and follow approved onboarding, access, transfer and exit processes. |
| **Personnel and contractors** | Comply with applicable requirements, protect credentials and information, complete assigned training and report suspected incidents. |
| **Supplier / relationship owners** | Ensure third-party due diligence, contractual requirements, access, monitoring and exit obligations are managed. |
| **Internal auditors** | Perform impartial audit activities under an approved audit programme; avoid auditing their own work. |

## 6. Mandatory Policy Requirements

### 6.1 Risk management

Information security risks shall be identified, analyzed, evaluated and treated under the approved risk methodology. Risk criteria, likelihood and impact definitions, acceptance authority, treatment options, review triggers and recordkeeping shall be documented. Risk acceptance shall be explicit, attributable to an authorized risk owner, and reviewed when assumptions or risk conditions materially change.

### 6.2 Asset ownership and information classification

In-scope information assets shall have assigned ownership appropriate to their nature and criticality. The organization shall define classification and handling rules, including requirements for access, storage, transmission, sharing, retention and secure disposal. The detailed classification scheme and handling rules shall be maintained in controlled supporting documents.

### 6.3 Identity and access management

Access shall be based on authorized business need and least privilege. Provisioning, modification, privileged access, periodic review and revocation shall follow approved procedures. Authentication requirements, access approval authorities, segregation-of-duties checks and review frequency shall be defined and evidenced. Shared or emergency access shall be subject to explicit controls and monitoring.

### 6.4 Secure operation and change

Systems and services shall be operated and changed under approved procedures proportionate to risk and criticality. The organization shall define requirements for secure configuration, vulnerability management, logging, monitoring, backup, recovery, change authorization and separation of duties where applicable. Exceptions shall be documented and approved before implementation except where an approved emergency process applies.

### 6.5 Incident management

Suspected information security events shall be reported through designated channels, assessed and escalated under the incident management procedure. The process shall address classification, response authority, evidence preservation, communications, recovery, lessons learned and corrective actions. Any statutory, regulatory or contractual notification requirement and deadline shall be assessed against the actual incident facts and verified applicable obligations.

### 6.6 Supplier and third-party security

Third-party risks shall be assessed according to service criticality, information sensitivity, access, processing activity and dependency. Applicable contractual controls, incident notification, subcontracting, assurance evidence, continuity, access termination and return or deletion of information shall be addressed where relevant.

### 6.7 Personnel awareness and responsibilities

Personnel shall receive information security responsibilities appropriate to their role and shall complete assigned awareness or training activities. Acknowledgement records shall identify the document version and authenticated person where feasible. Acknowledgement alone shall not be treated as proof of comprehension or effective behavior.

### 6.8 Business continuity and resilience

Continuity and recovery requirements shall be based on business impact, risk and approved recovery objectives. Backup, restoration and continuity arrangements shall be tested at defined intervals, with results, failures, corrective actions and retests recorded.

### 6.9 Legal, regulatory and contractual obligations

The organization shall maintain a process to identify, validate, assign ownership to, and periodically review obligations relevant to its services, locations, data, customers and suppliers. Interpretations and applicability decisions shall be recorded. This policy does not itself determine legal applicability.

## 7. Control Implementation and Evidence

| Governance Object | Minimum Expected Record | Assurance Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Risk ID, scenario, owner, assessment, treatment, acceptance, review date | A completed register does not by itself prove risks are adequately assessed. |
| **Control** | Control ID, owner, implementation status, procedure, evidence, test result | A declared implementation status is not proof of operating effectiveness. |
| **Exception** | Requirement, justification, risk, approver, safeguards, expiry/review, closure | Approval does not eliminate the underlying risk. |
| **Incident** | Timeline, classification, actions, evidence, decisions, closure and lessons learned | A closed ticket alone does not prove complete response or required notification. |
| **Training / acknowledgement** | Person, document version, timestamp, outcome and record integrity | A record does not prove understanding or compliance. |
| **Internal audit finding** | Criteria, evidence, finding, owner, root cause, action, due date, retest | A corrective action is not effective until appropriately verified. |
