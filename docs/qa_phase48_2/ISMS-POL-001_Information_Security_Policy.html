1. Purpose

This policy establishes management direction and mandatory principles for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and supporting the organization’s information security management system (ISMS). It sets expectations for governance, risk-based decision-making, implementation, monitoring and continual improvement.

2. Scope and Applicability

The organization shall define the ISMS scope in its approved scope statement. This policy applies to personnel, processes, information, technology, facilities and third parties to the extent they fall within that scope or interact with in-scope information assets. The organization shall document relevant interfaces, dependencies and exclusions rather than assuming universal applicability.

3. Terms and Definitions

TermWorking Definition (Model)
Information securityPreservation of the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.
ISMSThe policies, processes, roles, resources and controls the organization uses to establish, operate, monitor and improve information security within its approved scope.
Risk ownerThe person or role with accountability and authority to manage an assigned risk, including treatment decisions and acceptance within delegated limits.
Control ownerThe person or role responsible for implementing, operating and evidencing an assigned control.
ExceptionA documented, risk-assessed and authorized deviation from a mandatory requirement that is time-bound or subject to periodic review.
NonconformityFailure to fulfil a requirement of this policy, a supporting controlled document, or another applicable obligation.
Corrective actionAction to eliminate the cause of a nonconformity and prevent recurrence, verified as effective before closure.
EvidenceAttributable, retrievable and protected records demonstrating that a requirement or control was performed as defined.
