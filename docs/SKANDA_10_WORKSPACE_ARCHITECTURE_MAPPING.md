## Executive Summary: The 10-Workspace Information Architecture

SKANDA GRC unifies all governance, risk, multi-framework compliance, audit assurance, third-party risk, cyber threat intelligence, and AI analytics into **10 cohesive business workspaces**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                               SKANDA GRC ENTERPRISE PLATFORM                           │
├───────────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬────────────────────────┬───────────────┤
│ 1. 🏠 HOME    │ 2. ✅ MY WORK │ 3. 🏢 GOV     │ 4. 🛡 COMPLIANCE       │ 5. ⚠️ RISK    │
│ Command Center│ Tasks, CAPA & │ Governance,   │ Multi-Standard         │ Enterprise    │
│ & Executive   │ Personal Queue│ Policies & Cl4│ Frameworks & Journeys  │ Calibration & │

│ 6. 📋 CTRL/EVD│ 7. 📑 AUDIT   │ 8. 🔗 TPRM    │ 9. 📊 REPORTS          │ 10. 🧠 SKANDA │
│ Controls &    │ Assurance,    │ Assets, CMDB &│ Board Briefings,       │ Intelligence, │
│ Evidence Vault│ CAPA, Gates & │ Third-Party   │ Heatmaps & Training    │ EASM, Threats │
```
